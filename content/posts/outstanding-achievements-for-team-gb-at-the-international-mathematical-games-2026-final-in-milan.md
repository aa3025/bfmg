We are thrilled to share the fantastic results of  🇬🇧Team GB 🇬🇧 at the 2-day <a href="https://giochimatematici.unibocconi.eu/international-final-championship-mathematical-and-logical-games" target="_blank" rel="noopener">International Mathematical  and Logical Games Final</a>, held in Bocconi University (Italy, Milan) on 25-26 July, 2026! Competing against top mathematical talent from around the globe, our team delivered exceptional performances across multiple categories, bringing home two 1st place champion titles and several top-10 finishes.
<h2>Highlights &amp; Individual Standings</h2>
<h3>CE// Category (Parallel)</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Samuel Stuart</strong> took <strong>1st Place</strong> in the world with a score of 10 solved puzzles in just 43 minutes!</li>
 	<li><strong>Yison Li</strong> delivered a strong performance, finishing in <strong>5th Place</strong> (8 solved)!</li>
</ul>
<h3>CM Category</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Monica Stuart</strong> earned an impressive <strong>10th Place</strong> finish in competitive 63-contestants group, solving 15 puzzles!</li>
</ul>
<h3>C1 Category</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Yusuf Shah</strong> secured <strong>1st Place</strong> globally amongst 88 contestants with a maximum score of 22 solved puzzles in amazing 120 minutes!</li>
</ul>
<h3>C1// Category (Parallel)</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Solomon Stuart</strong> placed <strong>4th</strong> with 21 solved puzzles in 130 minutes!.</li>
</ul>
<h3>L1 Category</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Anoushka Bouri</strong> placed <strong>38th</strong> in a highly competitive division, solving 24 puzzles!</li>
</ul>
<h3>L2// Category (Parallel)</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Josh Turley</strong> achieved a fantastic <strong>7th Place</strong> finish with 27 solved puzzles!</li>
</ul>
<h3>GP// Category (Parallel)</h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Rico Li</strong> represented GB in the General Public parallel category, placing <strong>19th</strong> with 23 solved puzzles!</li>
</ul>
A huge congratulations  🎉 to all our finalists for their incredible logic, speed, and dedication on the international stage!
