Dear UK Logic and Mathematical Games 2025 Participants,

Thank you for registering to take part in 2025 Games at Coventry!

------

<strong>Place</strong>: <a href="https://eur01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmaps.app.goo.gl%2FXFdvF94SFGTzHJ2e9&amp;data=05%7C02%7CMathsGames-BFMG-Info%40live.coventry.ac.uk%7C97ce41cca0784776279b08dd8645fd44%7C4b18ab9a37654abeac7c0e0d398afd4f%7C0%7C0%7C638814358135473014%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&amp;sdata=74xhn1wd4S7sc7bSo7bp6mhQgSqXTUn0cdPZfZWOfps%3D&amp;reserved=0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Coventry University, Frank Whittle (FW) building (Faculty of Engineering Environment and Science)</a>

<strong>Parking</strong>: You will be able to use <a href="https://eur01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmaps.app.goo.gl%2FXGx5CrQnkhiYj1M5A&amp;data=05%7C02%7CMathsGames-BFMG-Info%40live.coventry.ac.uk%7C97ce41cca0784776279b08dd8645fd44%7C4b18ab9a37654abeac7c0e0d398afd4f%7C0%7C0%7C638814358135485715%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&amp;sdata=XQU6lqku9desltGMHpA46rl5Wwq6VWKDr5wYtZHwpiY%3D&amp;reserved=0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CU Student Car Park</a> for free, print the attached parking permit and display behind the windscreen (if you didn't get it, you will get one at the Reception to FW building). The car park is behind the Beatrice Shilling building off Gulson road. See screenshot of the map on how to get from parking to FW building.

<strong>Programme</strong>:

12:00 Arrivals and registration (parking permit attached to e-mails which were sent)
12:45 Everyone should be seated (ECG-24 Lecture theatre)
13:00 Start of the competition in ECG-24 Lecture theatre
16:00 Finish of the competition (180 min max, depending on your category you may finish earlier)
16:00-16:30 Marking of the results
16:45 – 17:00 Announcement of results

The number of problems and timings for different categories as well as links to past papers are available on our website: <a href="../index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">../index.html</a>

Looking Forward seeing you at the Games!
BFMG Organising Committee
