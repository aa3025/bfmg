Dear All

The International Final will take place on <strong>Saturday, August 23rd and Sunday, August 24th, 2025</strong>, in the city of <strong>Mahdia, Tunisia</strong> at the Faculty of Economics and Management of Mahdia.

Mahdia is a coastal city located 208 km from the capital Tunis (which has the Tunis-Carthage International Airport) and 40 km from the city of Monastir (which has the Habib Bourguiba International Airport). The Local Organising Committee (LOC) has scheduled to welcome participants starting from <strong>Thursday, August 21st</strong>, and departures are planned for <strong>Monday, August 25th, 2025</strong>.
