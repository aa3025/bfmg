<p class="Standard">The 2024 final of the Logic and Mathematical Games will take place on the campus of Coventry University, CV1 2JH, on <strong>Saturday, 25<sup>th</sup> May 2024</strong>, from <strong>1pm to 4pm</strong> (time and number of problems vary by category).</p>

<div>The schedule for May 25<sup>th</sup> is:</div>
<div>11:00-12:45 arrival, registration;</div>
<div>13:00-16:00 competitions;</div>
<div>from 17:00 or 17:30 rankings, closing ceremony.</div>
<div></div>
Can <strong>you</strong> solve these questions and puzzles?

&nbsp;

<p class="Standard">Participants around the world scoring high enough will qualify for the International Final, at its 38th edition in 2024, to be held on 25-26 August in <strong>Paris, France</strong>!</p>
<p class="Standard"><strong>To participate register here: </strong>https://forms.office.com/e/GxAwfTLwBu</p>
<p class="Standard">For further information contact us here: https://forms.office.com/e/6XyWriHgHT</p>
We also organise weekly logic and math problem solving sessions at Coventry Uni. These are informal, if you would like to participate let us know at the above address.
<p class="Standard">We look forward to seeing you!</p>
<strong>Categories:</strong>
<div>CHILDREN</div>
<ul>
 	<li>CE    Year 5 students     5 problems, max. 60 minutes</li>
 	<li>CM    Year 6-7 students     8 problems, max. 90 minutes</li>
 	<li>C1    Year 8-9 students     11 problems, max. 120 minutes</li>
 	<li>C2    Year 10-11 students     14 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
<div></div>
<div>TEENS AND SCHOLARS</div>
<ul>
 	<li>L1    Post-mandatory school students     16 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
 	<li>L2    University students     18 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
<div></div>
<div>ADULTS</div>
<ul>
 	<li>GP    Adults, general public     16 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
 	<li>HC    Adults, top-level competition     18 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
&nbsp;

<strong>Rules:</strong>

For ranking purposes, in each category, in order or priority:
<ul>
 	<li>1) most correctly solved problems;</li>
 	<li>2) if tied, most points;</li>
 	<li>3) if still tied, least time;</li>
 	<li>4) if still tied, youngest participant first.</li>
</ul>
No calculators, computers, phones, etc.

<strong>Allowed material:</strong> pens, pencils, erasers, rulers, protractors, compasses, scissors.
