We are academics at the University of Coventry, and enthusiastic about mathematical games!

These are widespread in other countries, with tens of thousands of participants every year.

The first edition of the British Final took place at the University of Essex, in 2022, and the second edition at the University of Coventry, in 2023. Last year, for the first time British participants qualified for the International Final!

Our long term goal is to broaden interest for the games in the UK across schools, universities, and the general public. In general, no specialist knowledge is required to solve, but a lot of creativity will certainly help!

&nbsp;

Who can take part in Logic &amp; Mathematical Games? -- Everyone! You can participate in one of these categories:

<strong>Categories:</strong>
<div>CHILDREN</div>
<ul>
 	<li>CE    Year 5 students     5 problems, max. 60 minutes</li>
 	<li>CM    Year 6-7 students     8 problems, max. 90 minutes</li>
 	<li>C1    Year 8-9 students     11 problems, max. 120 minutes</li>
 	<li>C2    Year 10-11 students     14 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
<div></div>
<div>TEENS AND SCHOLARS</div>
<ul>
 	<li>L1    Post-mandatory school students     16 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
 	<li>L2    University students     18 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
<div></div>
<div>ADULTS</div>
<ul>
 	<li>GP    Adults, general public     16 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
 	<li>HC    Adults, top-level competition     18 problems, max. 180 minutes</li>
</ul>
&nbsp;

<strong>Rules:</strong>

For ranking purposes, in each category, in order or priority:
<ul>
 	<li>1) most correctly solved problems;</li>
 	<li>2) if tied, most points;</li>
 	<li>3) if still tied, least time;</li>
 	<li>4) if still tied, youngest participant first.</li>
</ul>
No calculators, computers, phones, etc.

<strong>Allowed material:</strong> pens, pencils, erasers, rulers, protractors, compasses, scissors.
