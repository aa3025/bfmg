# Solution: 35_30_p9 - Black Squares

**Answer: 6 columns**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Define the column counts
Let `x`, `y`, and `z` be the numbers of columns with 1, 2, and 3 black squares.  Then `x = 4` and `x + y + z = 15`.

### Step 2 - Count all black squares
`x + 2y + 3z = 32`. Subtracting the first equation gives `y + 2z = 17`; with `y + z = 11`, this gives `z = 6`.
