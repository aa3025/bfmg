# Solution: 35_30_p11 - The Zoo

**Answer: 3, 5, 7, or 9 dromedaries**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Count animals from hooves
Sixty hooves mean there are 15 animals.  Let the numbers of camels, dromedaries, and donkeys be `c`, `d`, and `n`; then `c + d + n = 15`.

### Step 2 - Use the hump total
The hump count is `2c + d = 17`, so `d = 17 - 2c`.  Also `n = 15 - c - d = c - 2`, so `c` must be from 4 to 7.  This gives `d = 9, 7, 5, 3`, respectively: **3, 5, 7, or 9** dromedaries.
