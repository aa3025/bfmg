# Solution: 35_30_p4 - Gears

**Answer: 12 complete rotations**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Relate turns to tooth counts
Meshed gears pass the same number of teeth.  Ten turns of A pass `10 x 30 = 300` teeth to B.

### Step 2 - Continue to gear C
B therefore turns `300 / 18` times and passes 300 teeth to C.  Thus C turns `300 / 25 = 12` times.
