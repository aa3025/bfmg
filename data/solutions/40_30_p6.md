# Solution: 40_30_p6 — Matilda’s Pictures

**Answer: 24 pictures**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Let the total number be n
Matilda glues the first half, n/2 pictures, at 1 picture per day. This takes **n/2 days**.

She glues the second half, again n/2 pictures, at 3 pictures per day. This takes **n/6 days**.

### Step 2 – Use the total time
The two parts take 16 days in total:

**n/2 + n/6 = 16.**

Combining the fractions gives **2n/3 = 16**, hence:

**n = 24.**

Therefore Matilda had **24 pictures**.
