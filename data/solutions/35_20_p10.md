# Solution: 35_20_p10 — Eight Divisors

**Answer: 24**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Check Lisa’s observation
The sum from 1 to 21 is:

**1 + 2 + ··· + 21 = 21 × 22 ÷ 2 = 231.**

Since **231 = 3 × 7 × 11**, each of the three prime factors can either be included or omitted in a divisor. Therefore it has **2 × 2 × 2 = 8 divisors**.

### Step 2 – Use the divisor-count rule
If a number has prime factorisation

**pᵃ × qᵇ × ···**, 

then its number of positive divisors is **(a + 1)(b + 1)···**. To obtain exactly 8 divisors, the possible exponent patterns are:

| Prime-factor pattern | Example of its smallest number | Divisor count |
|---|---:|---:|
| p⁷ | 2⁷ = 128 | 8 |
| p³q | 2³ × 3 = 24 | (3 + 1)(1 + 1) = 8 |
| pqr | 2 × 3 × 5 = 30 | 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 |

### Step 3 – Choose the smallest possibility
The smallest number in the three cases is **24**. Its divisors are:

**1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24**,

so it has exactly eight divisors.
