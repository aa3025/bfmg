# Solution: 35_30_p2 - Carpenter's Rule

**Answer: 2 different triangle shapes**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Express the side lengths
Measure each side in 20-cm segments.  The two folds split the ten segments into three positive whole-number side lengths `a <= b <= c`, with `a + b + c = 10`.

### Step 2 - Apply the triangle inequality
A genuine triangle needs `a + b > c`; equality would be flattened.  Checking the partitions of 10 gives `(1, 4, 5)` and `(2, 3, 5)` as impossible or flat, while `(3, 3, 4)` and `(2, 4, 4)` work.  Hence there are **2** shapes.
