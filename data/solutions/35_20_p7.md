# Solution: 35_20_p7 — The ConsecYears

**Answer: 17990**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Define ConsecYears
A **ConsecYear** is a number formed by writing two consecutive whole numbers side by side. Examples:
- 78 (from 7 and 8)
- 2021 (from 20 and 21)
- Note: numbers cannot start with 0, so 910 (from 9 and 10) is valid, but we'd exclude e.g. 01.

### Step 2 – List all ConsecYears from 12 to 2021
ConsecYears in this range are formed by joining *n* and *n+1* where the result is between 12 and 2021:

| n | n+1 | ConsecYear |
|---|-----|-----------|
| 1 | 2 | 12 |
| 2 | 3 | 23 |
| 3 | 4 | 34 |
| ... | ... | ... |
| 8 | 9 | 89 |
| 9 | 10 | 910 |
| 10 | 11 | 1011 |
| ... | ... | ... |
| 20 | 21 | 2021 |

Total: **20 ConsecYears** (n = 1 through 20).

### Step 3 – Sum them all
Adding all 20 ConsecYears: 12 + 23 + 34 + 45 + 56 + 67 + 78 + 89 + 910 + 1011 + 1112 + 1213 + 1314 + 1415 + 1516 + 1617 + 1718 + 1819 + 1920 + 2021

Group the 2-digit ones: 12+23+34+45+56+67+78+89 = **404**
Group the 4-digit ones (n=9 to n=20, twelve numbers): 910+1011+...+2021

Sum of 4-digit ConsecYears = 910 + (1011+1112+...+2021)
= 910 + sum of 11 terms from 1011 to 2021
= 910 + 11 × (1011+2021)/2 = 910 + 11 × 1516 = 910 + 16676 = **17586**

Total = 404 + 17586 = **17990**
