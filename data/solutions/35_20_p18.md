# Solution: 35_20_p18 — Sharing the Garden

**Answer: BD = 27 m and BE = 40 m**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Use the equal-perimeter condition
Let **BD = x** and **BE = y**. The small triangle BDE has perimeter x + y + DE. The remaining quadrilateral has perimeter (36 − x) + (60 − y) + DE + 38.

Equating the perimeters cancels DE:

**x + y = 36 − x + 60 − y + 38**,

so **x + y = 67**.

### Step 2 – Use the equal-area condition
Triangles BDE and BAC have the same angle at B. Their area ratio is therefore:

**area(BDE) / area(BAC) = (BD × BE) / (BA × BC).**

The areas are equal, so this ratio is 1/2. Since BA = 36 and BC = 60:

**xy = 36 × 60 ÷ 2 = 1080.**

### Step 3 – Solve for the two distances
The two distances have sum 67 and product 1080, so they are roots of:

**t² − 67t + 1080 = 0 = (t − 27)(t − 40).**

Thus the two values are 27 and 40. Since D lies on AB, BD must be at most 36, so:

**BD = 27 m and BE = 40 m.**
