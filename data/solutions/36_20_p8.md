# Solution: 36_20_p8 - 3 Squares on a Rectangle

**Answer: 132 cm²**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Find each square side
Each square has area 22 cm², so its side is `sqrt(22)` cm.

### Step 2 - Use the aligned layout
The midpoint conditions fix the rectangle as three square-sides by two square-sides.  Its area is `6 x 22 =` **132 cm²**.
