# Solution: 35_30_p14 - Augmentation Operation

**Answer: -61 or 29**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Add the same number x
The corrected operation is `(12+x)(21+x)=2021+x`.

### Step 2 - Solve the quadratic
Expanding gives `252 + 33x + x^2 = 2021 + x`, hence `x^2 + 32x - 1769 = 0`.  This factors as `(x-29)(x+61)=0`, so `x = 29` or `x = -61`.  Directly, `41 x 50 = 2050` and `-49 x -40 = 1960` confirm both.
