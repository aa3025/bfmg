# Solution: 36_20_p5 - Cannonballs

**Answer: 36 contact points**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Count within each layer
The 3-by-3 base contributes 12 horizontal contacts; the 2-by-2 layer contributes 4.

### Step 2 - Count contacts between layers
Each of the 4 middle balls touches 4 below it (16 contacts), and the top ball touches all 4 middle balls.  Total: `12+4+16+4 = 36`.
