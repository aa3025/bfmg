# Solution: 40_30_p5 — Light Year

**Answer: 25 matches**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Identify the fixed digits
Every year from 2000 to 2099 begins with **20**. In the displayed match-stick style, 2 uses 5 matches and 0 uses 6, so these fixed digits use:

**5 + 6 = 11 matches.**

### Step 2 – Maximise the final two digits
Among the digit shapes shown, 8 uses the most matches: **7**. To be able to write every possible pair of final digits, we need enough matches for the most demanding year, which is **2088**.

### Step 3 – Count the matches for 2088
The year 2088 needs:

**5 + 6 + 7 + 7 = 25 matches.**

The extra year 2100 needs only 22 matches, so it does not increase the maximum. Therefore the minimum number required is **25**.
