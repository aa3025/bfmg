# Solution: 35_30_p13 - The Jewel Box

**Answer: 5 cm, 8 cm, or 12 cm**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Set up the dimensions
Let the square faces be `s x s` and the length be `l`.  Volume equals area when `s^2 l = 2s^2 + 4sl`.

### Step 2 - Use whole-number dimensions
Rearranging gives `l(s-4)=2s`, so `l = 2s/(s-4) = 2 + 8/(s-4)`.  Thus `s-4` must be a positive divisor of 8.  The possibilities are `s=5, 6, 8, 12`; `s=6` gives `l=6`, making all faces square, so it is excluded.  The possible sides are **5 cm, 8 cm, and 12 cm**.
