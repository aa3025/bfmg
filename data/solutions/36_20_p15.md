# Solution: 36_20_p15 - This Year's Stars

**Answer: 335**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Choose a step size
A regular star on 2022 vertices is determined by a step `k`, with `1 < k < 1011`.

### Step 2 - Exclude disconnected drawings
The drawing is one star exactly when `gcd(k,2022)=1`.  Counting the eligible step sizes up to reversal gives `phi(2022)/2 =` **335**.
