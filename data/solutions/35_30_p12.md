# Solution: 35_30_p12 - A Remarkable Year

**Answer: 1821**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Write the place-value equation
Using place value, the equation is `(1000A+100B+10C+D) + (100A+10B+C) + (10A+B) = 2021`.  Therefore `1110A + 111B + 11C + D = 2021`.

### Step 2 - Resolve from right to left
The thousands digit forces `A=1`.  Subtracting 1110 leaves `911 = 111B + 11C + D`.  Taking `B=8` leaves 23, which is `11 x 2 + 1`; hence `C=2` and `D=1`.  The year is **1821**.
