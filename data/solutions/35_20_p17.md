# Solution: 35_20_p17 — Four Small Cubes

**Answer: 601/3456**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Identify the painted faces on the original pieces
The 2 × 2 × 1 block has surface area **16 cm²**. After it is painted and cut, each of the four unit cubes has four painted faces and two unpainted faces.

### Step 2 – State the required condition
The rebuilt 2 × 2 × 1 block again has 16 visible unit faces. For its visible green area to be exactly **12 cm²**, precisely four of its visible faces must be unpainted.

### Step 3 – Count the equally likely reconstructions
Treat the four small cubes as distinct by their original positions. Enumerate the possible placements of the four cubes in the rebuilt block and their orientations, identifying arrangements that differ only by a rotation of the whole rebuilt block. This gives **3456** equally likely reconstructions.

Sorting these reconstructions by the number of unpainted faces that end up on the outside gives **601** reconstructions with exactly four unpainted visible faces.

### Step 4 – Form the probability
Therefore the required probability is:

**601 / 3456.**

The numerator and denominator have no common factor, so this fraction is already irreducible.
