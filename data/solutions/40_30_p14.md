**2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5, 5.5 litres.**

### Step 2 – Read the fuel gauge

**2 + 2.5 + 3 = 7.5 litres.**

### Step 3 – Subtract the fuel used on the trip

**80 × 6 ÷ 100 = 4.8 litres.**

Thus the fuel left is **7.5 − 4.8 = 2.7 litres**.
