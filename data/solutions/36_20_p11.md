# Solution: 36_20_p11 - A Total of a Year

**Answer: 81**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Find the total to subtract
`1+2+...+100 = 5050`.  To obtain 2022, the numbers with minus signs must total `(5050-2022)/2 = 1514`.

### Step 2 - Delay the first minus sign
Keep 1 through 80 positive.  Starting with 81, select numbers totalling 1514; this is possible, while a first minus sign after 81 is not.  The first is **81**.
