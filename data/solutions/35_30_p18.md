# Solution: 35_30_p18 - Duke Hunter's Wetland

**Answer: 315 dam**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Use the cyclic quadrilateral condition
For a cyclic quadrilateral with perpendicular diagonals, express the four side lengths using the two diagonals and the angles at their intersection.

### Step 2 - Maximise the area under the two side-sum conditions
Imposing opposite-side sums of 360 dam and 450 dam, then maximising the area, determines the largest side.  Rounded to the nearest decametre it is **315 dam**.
