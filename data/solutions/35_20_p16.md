# Solution: 35_20_p16 — Kryptonian Square

**Answer: 117**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Use a property of every 3 × 3 magic square
Let the centre entry be c and the common row sum be M. In a 3 × 3 magic square, opposite entries add to 2c, so each row sums to **3c**. Hence M = 3c and the total of all nine entries is:

**3M = 9c.**

Thus we only need to decode the centre entry.

### Step 2 – Decode the base
Use the given glyphs for 0 and 9, together with the three consecutive glyphs, in the row, column, and diagonal equations. Solving these equations gives a positional base of **13**. The centre cell is written as **10** in Kryptonian notation, so its value in base 10 is **13**.

### Step 3 – Find the total
The total of the nine entries is nine times the centre value:

**9 × 13 = 117.**
