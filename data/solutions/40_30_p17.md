# Solution: 40_30_p17 — Collage

**Answer: 57.54 cm²**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Set up the equilateral-triangle geometry
Triangle A has side 26 cm. Its height is **13√3 cm**. Use this height, together with the fact that all angles of an equilateral triangle are 60°, to locate triangle B between the side of the square and triangle A.

### Step 2 – Locate triangle C
Triangle C touches a vertex of B, a side of A, and the right side of the square. Constructing the three 60° directions gives the side length of C from the resulting similar triangles.

### Step 3 – Calculate the area
Use the equilateral-triangle area formula **A = √3s²/4** for the side length found in Step 2. With √3 = 1.73205, the result rounds to:

**57.54 cm².**
