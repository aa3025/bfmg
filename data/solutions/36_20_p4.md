# Solution: 36_20_p4 - Matilda's Number

**Answer: 36**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Translate the condition
For digits `a` and `b`, the rule is `ab = 2(a+b)`.

### Step 2 - Find the smallest solution
Rearrange to `(a-2)(b-2)=4`.  The smallest two-digit solution comes from `a-2=1`, `b-2=4`, giving **36**.
