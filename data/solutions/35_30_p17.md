# Solution: 35_30_p17 - So many triangles!

**Answer: 2700 triangles**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Translate the condition
An integer-angle triangle is determined, up to reflection, by an unordered triple of positive integers whose sum is 180.

### Step 2 - Count the partitions
For a fixed smallest angle `a`, the middle angle `b` ranges from `a` to `floor((180-a)/2)`, and `c=180-a-b`.  Summing those counts for `a=1` to `60` gives 2700.  Each unordered triple is counted once, so the answer is **2700**.
