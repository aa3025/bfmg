# Solution: 35_20_p11 — Gift Wrapping

**Answer: 2 solutions: 81 cm or 137 cm**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Describe the dimensions of the box
Let the side length of the square top and bottom be **s** cm, and let the height be **h** cm. The volume condition is:

**s²h = 225.**

All dimensions must be whole numbers greater than 1.

### Step 2 – Find the possible box dimensions
We need a square divisor of 225. Since **225 = 3² × 5²**, the possible values of s greater than 1 are:

| Square side s | Height h = 225 ÷ s² | Valid? |
|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 25 | yes |
| 5 | 9 | yes |
| 15 | 1 | no: the height must be greater than 1 |

So the two possible boxes have dimensions **3 × 3 × 25** and **5 × 5 × 9**.

### Step 3 – Calculate the ribbon around the box
The picture shows two perpendicular loops of ribbon. Each loop goes around a rectangle of dimensions s by h, so its length is **2(s + h)**. The two loops together use:

**4(s + h) cm.**

The knot then adds 25 cm.

### Step 4 – Find both total lengths
For the 3 × 3 × 25 box:

**4(3 + 25) + 25 = 112 + 25 = 137 cm.**

For the 5 × 5 × 9 box:

**4(5 + 9) + 25 = 56 + 25 = 81 cm.**

Therefore Emil needs **81 cm or 137 cm** of ribbon.
