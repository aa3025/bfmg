# Solution: 40_30_p15 — CCTV

**Answer: 30 minutes**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Calculate Axel’s remaining viewing time
Axel has 2 h 30 min = 150 minutes of video left. At 1.5 times normal speed, his remaining real time is:

**150 ÷ 1.5 = 100 minutes.**

### Step 2 – Calculate Bob’s remaining viewing time
Bob has 5 h 25 min = 325 minutes of video left. At 2.5 times normal speed, his remaining real time is:

**325 ÷ 2.5 = 130 minutes.**

### Step 3 – Compare the finishing times
Bob finishes **130 − 100 = 30 minutes** after Axel.
