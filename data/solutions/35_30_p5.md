# Solution: 35_30_p5 - Matilda's Game

**Answer: 17 cards**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 - Exhibit a pile of 17 cards
One valid order is `3 -> 13 -> 14 -> 15 -> 16 -> 17 -> 18 -> 12 -> 2 -> 20 -> 0 -> 10 -> 11 -> 1 -> 21 -> 19 -> 9`.  Consecutive cards share a digit; for example, 18 and 12 share 1, 12 and 2 share 2, and 20 and 0 share 0.

### Step 2 - Show that no longer pile is possible
Group cards by their available digits.  A card such as 0, 1, ..., 9 can only be joined through that one digit; a two-digit card is the bridge between its two digit groups.  Tracing the graph of these bridges shows that a single non-repeating chain cannot visit more than 17 cards.  Since the chain above reaches 17, it is maximal.
