# Solution: 35_20_p4 — Eight-sided Die

**Answer: 7**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Find the constant opposite-pair sum
An octahedron has **8 triangular faces** forming **4 pairs of opposite faces**. The numbers 1 to 8 are placed one per face, with every opposite pair summing to the same value S.

The total of all numbers: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 = **36**

Since there are 4 opposite pairs each summing to S: **4 × S = 36**, so **S = 9**.

### Step 2 – List the 4 opposite pairs
Every pair of opposite faces must sum to 9:
- 1 ↔ 8
- 2 ↔ 7
- 3 ↔ 6
- 4 ↔ 5

### Step 3 – Identify the shaded face's opposite
From the net in the figure, face **2** is placed just below face **1** in the centre of the cross-shaped unfolding. When the net is folded into an octahedron, the shaded face (at the bottom of the net) ends up **opposite to face 2**.

### Step 4 – Calculate the answer
Since opposite faces sum to 9 and the shaded face is opposite face 2:

**Shaded face = 9 − 2 = 7**
