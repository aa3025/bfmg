# Solution: 40_30_p3 — Cecily’s Cards

**Answer: 12 different numbers**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Consider the possible sets of visible digits
The 3 card always reads 3. Each of the other two cards can be read as either 6 or 9 by rotating it. Thus the possible multisets of digits are:

| Visible digits | Different arrangements |
|---|---:|
| 3, 6, 9 | 6 |
| 3, 6, 6 | 3 |
| 3, 9, 9 | 3 |

### Step 2 – Add the arrangements
With three different digits there are 3! = 6 arrangements. With two equal digits, there are 3 arrangements in each case.

Therefore the number of different numbers is:

**6 + 3 + 3 = 12.**
