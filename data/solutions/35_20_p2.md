# Solution: 35_20_p2 — Darts

**Answer: 17**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Understand the scoring system
The target has three zones. Each dart scores:
- **9** if it hits the bull (black centre)
- **3** if it hits the middle ring (grey)
- **1** if it hits the outer ring (white)
- **0** if it misses the target entirely

Matthew throws exactly **4 darts**, each scoring independently. The question asks for the **smallest total he cannot possibly achieve**.

### Step 2 – List all achievable totals
We need every total T that can be written as 9a + 3b + 1c + 0d, where a + b + c + d = 4 and a, b, c, d ≥ 0.

### Step 3 – Spot the first gap
Scanning from 1 upward: 1 through 16 are all achievable. **17 is missing.**

To score 17 you would need at least one 9: 9 + 8 from 3 remaining darts. But with scores of only 0, 1, 3, the maximum from 3 darts is 3 + 3 + 3 = **9**, not 8. So 17 is impossible.

With two 9s: 18 + anything ≥ 18 ≠ 17. With no 9s: maximum is 4 × 3 = 12 < 17.

### Step 4 – Confirm 17 cannot be made
Every route to 17 fails:
- 0 nines: max = 12 ✗
- 1 nine: needs 8 from 3 darts scoring from {0,1,3} — impossible (max = 9, but 8 is not reachable: 3+3+1=7, 3+3+3=9) ✗
- 2+ nines: minimum score ≥ 18 ✗

**17 is the smallest total Matthew cannot achieve.**
