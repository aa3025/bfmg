# Solution: 40_30_p4 — Horseshoe

**Answer: 28**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Choose two holes
There are 8 holes, and Mrs. Bonifer must choose 2 different holes for the nails. The order does not matter: using holes A and B is the same choice as using B and A.

### Step 2 – Count the pairs
The number of ways to choose 2 objects from 8 is:

**C(8, 2) = 8 × 7 ÷ 2 = 28.**

Therefore she can hang the horseshoe in **28 different ways**.
