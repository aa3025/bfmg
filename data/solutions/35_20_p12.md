# Solution: 35_20_p12 — Diagonals

**Answer: 50 regions**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Count regions made by chords
When all diagonals of a regular n-gon are drawn, no three diagonals meet at the same interior point. The number of regions inside the polygon is:

**1 + (number of diagonals) + (number of diagonal intersections).**

Each new diagonal creates one new region, plus one extra region for every interior intersection it crosses.

### Step 2 – Count the diagonals of a heptagon
There are **C(7, 2) = 21** segments joining pairs of vertices. Of these, 7 are sides, so the number of diagonals is:

**21 − 7 = 14.**

### Step 3 – Count interior intersections
An interior crossing is determined by choosing four vertices: their two crossing diagonals are the diagonals of that quadrilateral. Thus the number of intersections is:

**C(7, 4) = 35.**

### Step 4 – Find the total number of regions
Therefore the heptagon is split into:

**1 + 14 + 35 = 50 regions.**

As a check, the same method gives 1 + 5 + C(5, 4) = 11 for the pentagon in the question.
