# Solution: 40_30_p12 — Year 20 × 26

**Answer: 4 solutions: 2620, 4013, 5210, 6508**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Translate the condition
Write the four-digit year as ABCD, where AB is its first two-digit number and CD is its last two-digit number. We need:

**AB × CD = 20 × 26 = 520.**

### Step 2 – List suitable factor pairs
Both factors must fit in two digits. The factor pairs of 520 that give years later than 2026 are:

| AB | CD | Year |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 26 | 20 | 2620 |
| 40 | 13 | 4013 |
| 52 | 10 | 5210 |
| 65 | 8 | 6508 |

### Step 3 – Check completeness
The next possible factor pair would need a first factor above 99 or a last factor that is not a valid two-digit block for a four-digit year. Thus these are the only possibilities between 2027 and 9999.
