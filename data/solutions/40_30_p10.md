# Solution: 40_30_p10 — Chocolate Package

**Answer: 4 solutions: 1,2,7,8; 1,3,6,8; 1,4,6,7; 1,4,5,8**

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1 – Find the required total
The eight bags contain a total of:

**1 + 2 + ··· + 8 = 36 chocolates.**

Each child must receive half, or **18 chocolates**, and four bags. Marion must have bag 1, so her other three bags must total **17**.

### Step 2 – Find triples that total 17
Choose three distinct bag numbers from 2 to 8 with sum 17:

| Marion’s other bags | Marion’s four bags |
|---|---|
| 2, 7, 8 | 1, 2, 7, 8 |
| 3, 6, 8 | 1, 3, 6, 8 |
| 4, 6, 7 | 1, 4, 6, 7 |
| 4, 5, 8 | 1, 4, 5, 8 |

### Step 3 – Check the conditions
Each listed set has four bags and contains 18 chocolates. The remaining four bags therefore also contain 18 chocolates.

So there are **4 solutions**.
